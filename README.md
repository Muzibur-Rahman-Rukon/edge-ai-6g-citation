# Exploring the Integration of Edge AI into 6G Networks: Implications for Network Efficiency, Security, and Privacy

Official resource and citation repository for the IEEE ICCST 2025 paper.

## Abstract
The introduction of 6G networks is poised to deliver unprecedented advancements in wireless communication. However, realizing the full potential of 6G requires innovative solutions to inherent challenges in network efficiency, security, and privacy. This paper explores the integration of edge Artificial Intelligence (AI) into 6G networks as a transformative approach to move processing and intelligence closer to the data source...
